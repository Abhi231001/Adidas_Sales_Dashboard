# Dataset Details

Excel dataset contains Adidas sales records across multiple dimensions:

Time (Year, Month) → 2020 and 2021 sales data.

Sales Method → In-store, Online, Outlet.

Product Categories → Men’s Apparel, Men’s Athletic Footwear, Men’s Street Footwear, Women’s Apparel, Women’s Athletic Footwear, Women’s Street Footwear.

Retailers → Amazon, Foot Locker, Kohl’s, Sports Direct, Walmart, West Gear.

Metrics:

Units Sold

Sales Revenue

Operating Profit

Average Selling Price

This structure allows slicing and filtering by year, product, retailer, and sales channel.

# Chart

1. Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)
   
Purpose: Shows how sales fluctuate month by month across 2020–2021.

Insight: Peaks in 2020 reached $9.86M, while 2021 was steadier (max $7.78M). Useful for identifying seasonal demand patterns.

2. Profit by Region (Pie Chart)
   
Purpose: Displays contribution of each U.S. region to total profit.

Insight: The West (27%) is the most profitable region, followed by the Northeast (21%). Midwest contributes the least (16%).

3. Profit by Product (Bar Chart)
   
Purpose: Compares profitability across product categories.

Insight:

Men’s Street Footwear ($8.28M) is the top profit driver.

Women’s Apparel ($6.85M) is second.

Women’s Athletic Footwear ($3.89M) is the lowest.

This highlights which product lines are most lucrative.

4. Total Sales by Retailer (Horizontal Bar Chart)
   
Purpose: Shows which retailers generate the most sales revenue.

Insight:

West Gear ($24.20M) leads, followed by Foot Locker ($21.99M).

Amazon ($7.77M) and Walmart ($7.46M) contribute the least.

This helps evaluate retailer partnerships.

5. Sales by Gender (Treemap)
   
Purpose: Visualizes male vs female product sales distribution.

Insight: Male categories (street + athletic footwear, apparel) dominate slightly over female categories, showing Adidas’s stronger performance in men’s products.

6. Sales Method (Donut Chart)
   
Purpose: Breaks down sales by channel.

Insight:

Online (40%) is the largest channel ($35.66M).

Outlet (33%) and In-store (27%) follow.
This indicates Adidas’s growing reliance on e-commerce.
